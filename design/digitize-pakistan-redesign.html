Pakistan’s first national AI policy is here. What it means for you.

A national AI fund, data-residency rules and a target of a million trained professionals — we read all 60 pages.

Hamza QureshiPolicy editor · 23 Sep 2026 · 7 min read
ShareSave
article image · 16:9

The Ministry of IT & Telecom published the long-awaited framework on Monday evening. It is the first time the government has set out, in one place, how it expects artificial intelligence to be built, bought and regulated across the country.

Three things stand out. First, a national AI fund to back local research and startups. Second, rules on where Pakistanis’ data can be stored and processed. Third, a skills target that would, if met, make AI literacy as common as spreadsheet skills in the workforce.

What changes for working professionals

For most readers the immediate impact is on training. Employers in the public sector are expected to fund certified courses, and several private banks have already said they will follow.

“The policy is ambitious. The question, as always, is funding and follow-through.”
