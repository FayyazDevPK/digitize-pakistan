2a Direction A · full lockup system

The signal mark, in every place it has to live.

One geometry — the DP tile with the vermilion publishing dot — resolved into five lockups, three colorways, a bilingual variant, and hard rules for clear space and minimum size.

A1

Construction

everything derives from the tile unit u
THE UNIT

The tile is 46×46. u = 4 — the stroke weight of the D. Corner radius is 2.25u, cap height 5u, and the dot radius is 0.875u seated one u in from the lower-right corner.

THE DOT IS LOAD-BEARING

It breaks the tile's optical square, so the mark never reads as a generic app chiclet — and it's the only element allowed to animate (a 2s pulse on live-publishing surfaces).

TILE / GLYPH RATIO
glyph 43%

Glyph occupies 43% of the tile area. Never reflow it to fill more — the padding is what keeps it legible at 16px.

Draft national AI policy would require local data residency for public-sector models

Consultation closes in three weeks. Founders say the compliance bill falls hardest on pre-revenue teams.

Ayesha Kamal✓ VERIFIED✎ CREATOR
Policy desk, Karachi
ARTICLE IMAGE

The Ministry of IT and Telecom published a 46-page draft this week setting out how public-sector bodies may procure and deploy AI systems. The clause drawing the most attention requires that any model serving a government workload keep its training and inference data inside Pakistan.

For the handful of local teams already running inference on rented GPUs abroad, that would mean either migrating to domestic capacity that barely exists yet, or dropping out of public tenders entirely.

"We support residency in principle. We just can't be the ones who pay to build the data centre."

— founder, Karachi-based document AI startup
